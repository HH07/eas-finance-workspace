How this result was calculated
The result uses the figures you entered together with clearly identified modelling assumptions. Ratios and payment figures are calculated from those inputs. Market-position messages are broad lender-market indicators, not eligibility decisions, and do not reproduce any one lender's credit policy.
What this workspace does not decide
It does not approve finance, predict a lender's credit decision, provide a formal valuation, calculate tax or give legal advice. Actual lender criteria and pricing vary and should be checked against the current market.
